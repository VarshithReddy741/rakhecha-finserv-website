Our Story

Rakhecha Finserv has been helping individuals and corporates manage and grow their wealth for nearly a decade. With over 100 Crore in Assets Under Management (AUM), we specialize in providing the best risk-adjusted returns for our clients.

Starting as a mutual fund distributor, we have diversified our offerings to include a wide range of asset classes, detailed in our list of services. As a long-term oriented wealth management firm, we prioritize financial success over short-term gains.

Founded by two brothers with MBA degrees, Rakhecha Finserv aspires to become one of India's leading wealth management firms. Their expertise allows them to thoroughly evaluate and recommend the best funds, ensuring an excellent client experience.

At Rakhecha Finserv, we provide tailored solutions because we understand that investments, like clothes, do not fit the same for everyone.

Client satisfaction is our priority, as we believe that exceptional client experiences are the key to our success and growth through referrals.

To simplify complex wealth management by delivering research-driven asset allocation, proactive portfolio adaptation, and transparent client education. We commit to maximizing risk-adjusted returns while fostering a resilient community of informed, financially independent investors.
