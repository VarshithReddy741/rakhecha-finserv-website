  • Serve as the principal legal advisor to the Board of Directors, senior management, and business teams on legal, regulatory, governance, and strategic matters.
  • Ensure compliance with the Companies Act, RBI regulations, FEMA, labour laws, contract laws, data privacy laws, and other applicable statutory requirements.
  • Develop, implement, and maintain legal, compliance, governance, and risk management frameworks across the organization.
  • Drive corporate governance initiatives and advise the Board on governance best practices.
  • Oversee all corporate secretarial functions, including statutory registers, filings, board resolutions, annual filings, and regulatory submissions.
  • Plan and manage Board Meetings, Committee Meetings, Annual General Meetings (AGMs), and Extraordinary General Meetings (EGMs), including agenda preparation, board papers, minutes, and statutory documentation.

Contract Management

  • Draft, review, negotiate, and finalize commercial contracts, customer agreements, vendor agreements, partnership agreements, confidentiality agreements, technology agreements, employment contracts, and other legal documentation.
  • Ensure all contracts adequately protect the Company's commercial, legal, operational, and financial interests.
  • Standardize legal documentation and maintain contract governance across the organization.

Regulatory Compliance

  • Develop and oversee the Company's enterprise-wide compliance framework.
  • Ensure ongoing compliance with RBI regulations, NBFC regulatory requirements, AML/KYC/CFT guidelines, data protection laws, outsourcing guidelines, digital lending regulations, and other applicable regulatory frameworks.
  • Monitor regulatory developments and ensure timely implementation of new legal and compliance requirements.
