Leadership // Dev Piyush Rakhecha

Dev Piyush Rakhecha

Managing Partner & Chief Investment Officer
Connect on LinkedIn arrow_forward

Dev Piyush Rakhecha is the Managing Partner and Chief Investment Officer at Rakhecha Finserv, overseeing the firm's overarching investment strategy and advising ultra-high-net-worth families on complex wealth preservation tactics.

With more than 30 years of experience in leadership roles across global capital markets, operations, and private equity, Dev brings a profound understanding of macroeconomic trends and their impact on private portfolios. As CIO, he leads the firmwide investment committee; provides ongoing evaluation of the firm's strategic asset allocation models including overseeing multi-asset class investments; and provides significant insights on the financial implications of geopolitical shifts.

Before founding Rakhecha Finserv, Dev spent nearly two decades at premier global institutions where he was most recently Head of Global Wealth Management Strategy and managed a team of over 140 professionals in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and India. Prior to that she led a specialized 200-person team responsible for quantitative research, risk management, and regulatory compliance. Prior to this, he served in multiple executive leadership roles navigating volatile market cycles and steering institutional funds through rigorous growth phases.

He received his MBA from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania, and his BA in Economics from the London School of Economics.
